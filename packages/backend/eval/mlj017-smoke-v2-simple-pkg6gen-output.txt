## Dust Control and Silica Exposure Prevention

-   **Dust Control Measures:**
    -   Utilize wetting, HEPA-Vac, or green dust methods (p. 22).
    -   Implement and adhere to the approved Dust Control Plan (p. 22, 23).
    -   Stop work if visible dust is observed outside the barricaded area and correct the source (p. 23).
    -   Use wet methods to control airborne dust emissions or vacuuming methods as per 1926.1153 Table 1 (p. 22).
    -   Connect a water hose to the demo saw prior to operation, ensuring direct water supply or pressurized water tank (p. 23).
    -   Use wet methods (green dust or water) for sweeping during housekeeping to mitigate dust clouds (p. 24).
    -   Consider the IDA (Indeco Dust Abatement) System, which uses high-pressure water spray to prevent dust (p. 43).

-   **Respiratory Protection:**
    -   Employees will use N95 dust masks or ┬╜-face respirators (p. 22).
    -   Follow the approved Dust Control Plan for respiratory protection (p. 23).
    -   All workers conducting demolition must be medically cleared and fit-tested for respiratory protection (p. 22).
    -   Provide respiratory training and protection in accordance with 1926.1153 Table 1 (p. 22).

-   **Lead Exposure (for painted steel):**
    -   If painted steel is encountered, follow lead removal procedures before cutting (p. 23).
    -   All painted surfaces are considered to contain lead; implement lead placard protocols (e.g., 3P containment, respiratory protection) (p. 22).

-   **Containment:**
    -   Use fire retardant tarp and/or fire-rated plywood to contain debris (p. 22).
    -   Establish a work area with rigid barriers to keep the public away if the station/platform is open (p. 22, 7).

-   **General Safety:**
    -   Only trained and authorized workers are permitted to use saw cutting equipment (p. 22).
    -   Follow OSHA Silica Fact Sheet Attachment #1-2 (p. 7).
